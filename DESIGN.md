# Design System: Busy Season

## Overview

**Creative North Star: "The Contractor's Estimate"**

Busy Season looks the way a well-run trades business writes a quote: plain, confident, and honest about the numbers. There is no ornament, no agency flourish, no wordplay on the company name. Every screen makes one clear statement about an outcome and offers one action — "Get a Free Sample Ad" — and the money is typeset like it matters, never buried in fine print or set grey-on-grey. The visitor is a busy owner reading on a phone, often standing on a job site, with about five minutes; the design's whole job is to let that person see exactly what they get, what it costs, and what it costs all in, without hunting.

The system runs on high contrast and generous space rather than colour or decoration. Near-white cool paper (`#fafaf9`) is the default ground; deep charcoal (`#16181c`) carries the hero and the "what you get" and closing sections, where the site is allowed real drama. A single saturated amber is the only action colour on the page, and it is rationed — it fills the CTA and marks a step number, nothing else. Steel blue is the quieter supporting voice: eyebrows, labels, and the "Best value" emphasis on the Growth plan. Geometry is crisp — 6px corners, 1px hairline borders, an 8px grid — and surfaces are flat until something is deliberately lifted.

This is deliberately **not** the register of the founder's warmer consumer brand (a sister company in memorial/tribute services). That world uses serif type, a warm off-white, soft rounding, and a gentle, unhurried tone. Busy Season rejects all of it: no serif anywhere, cooler paper, sharper edges, higher contrast, a more saturated accent. A visitor who has seen both sites should never mistake one for the other, and nothing here should read as a creative agency selling art rather than a studio that understands small-business marketing.

**Key Characteristics:**
- One accent colour, one action per page — amber appears on ≤10% of any screen.
- Money is a first-class design element: prices, ad spend, and the all-in total get Space Grotesk weight, never muted grey.
- Charcoal sections for drama; cool paper for everything else; no third background mood.
- Crisp 6px geometry and 1px hairlines — no soft rounding, no heavy shadows at rest.
- No serif, ever. Space Grotesk for anything that carries weight, Inter for reading.
- Real client work is the imagery. Raw phone photos are welcome and stay unprettified.

## Colors

A cool, near-monochrome field — paper and charcoal doing almost all the work — with one saturated amber for action and a steel blue for support. Warmth is rationed to the amber alone.

### Primary
- **Work-Light Amber** (`#e8862e`): The single action colour. It fills the primary CTA button, the mobile-nav CTA, the step-number counters, the FAQ chevrons, and the `::selection` highlight — and essentially nothing else. On the near-white paper it is a **fill only, never text** (2.6:1, fails AA); the amber button therefore takes near-black label text (`#1a1b1e`, 7.3:1), not white. As *text* it is permitted only on charcoal, where it reaches 6.4:1 — that is why the all-in total flips from steel to amber inside dark sections.
- **Amber Pressed** (`#d4741d`): Hover/active state for the amber button and nav CTA. Never used as a fill or text colour in its own right.

### Secondary
- **Steel Blue** (`#3e5c76`): The supporting accent and the eyebrow/label colour on light grounds (6.9:1). It draws the 2px border and "Best value" badge on the featured Growth plan, tints that plan's per-unit pill, colours the all-in total on light sections, and marks the vertical workflow step numbers. It is the "trustworthy figure on a page" colour — quieter and more institutional than the amber.
- **Steel Pressed** (`#33506a`): Hover state for steel-blue interactive elements.

### Tertiary
- **Guarantee Green** (`#3f8f5f`): Reserved for the risk-reversal story. It fills the guarantee block's circular check mark, sets that block's headline and hairline borders (3.9:1 — large text and UI only, never body copy), and colours the check icons in pricing bullet lists and the success-notice border. It never appears outside a confirmation or guarantee context.

### Neutral
- **Cool Paper** (`#fafaf9`): The default page background. Cooler and cleaner than the sister brand's warm off-white — the coolness is load-bearing.
- **Paper Alt** (`#f2f1ef`): The alternating-band background for a light section that needs separation from its neighbours (`.section-alt`), and the per-unit pill fill on the non-featured plan.
- **Charcoal** (`#16181c`): The dark-section ground — hero, "what you get", closing CTA band, and footer. Where the site earns its drama.
- **Charcoal Soft** (`#1f2229`): Cards, inputs, and nested surfaces *inside* charcoal sections, so they separate from the ground without a border doing all the work.
- **Ink** (`#1a1b1e`): Primary text on light. Also the label colour on the amber button (see the contrast rule above).
- **Ink Muted** (`#5a5d63`): Secondary and supporting text on light (6.6:1 on paper) — ledes, card body, captions, nav links at rest.
- **On Dark** (`#f2f2f0`): Primary text on charcoal.
- **On Dark Dim** (`rgba(242, 242, 240, 0.68)`): Secondary text on charcoal — the dark-section equivalent of Ink Muted.
- **Hairline** (`#e2e2e0`): Borders and dividers on light — the 1px line that defines almost every card, input, table row, and section edge.
- **Hairline On Dark** (`rgba(242, 242, 240, 0.14)`): The same role on charcoal.

### Named Rules
**The One Voice Rule.** Amber is the only action colour and appears on no more than ~10% of any screen. If two things on a page are amber, one of them is wrong. Its scarcity is what makes the CTA unmissable.

**The Amber-Is-Not-Text Rule.** On any light background, amber may fill a shape but must never set type — it fails AA at 2.6:1. The amber button takes near-black text; white-on-amber is banned. Amber as text is allowed *only* on charcoal (6.4:1). Steel blue is the accent-coloured text on light. There is a comment block restating this at the top of `styles.css`; keep it.

## Typography

**Display Font:** Space Grotesk (with Inter, then system-ui, sans-serif) — loaded from Google Fonts at weights 500/600/700.
**Body Font:** Inter (with system-ui, -apple-system, Segoe UI, sans-serif) — weights 400/500/600.
**CJK fallback:** no CJK webfont. On the Simplified-Chinese pages Latin glyphs and digits stay in Space Grotesk / Inter; Han glyphs fall through per-glyph to a device system stack — `"PingFang SC", "HarmonyOS Sans SC", "Microsoft YaHei", "Noto Sans CJK SC", "Source Han Sans SC", system-ui`.

**Character:** A geometric-leaning grotesque paired with a workhorse UI sans. Space Grotesk gives headlines and numbers a confident, slightly mechanical edge; Inter keeps body copy quiet and legible at a phone's arm's length. Headlines run tight — negative tracking down to `-0.035em` on the hero — and `text-wrap: balance` keeps them from orphaning. **No serif appears anywhere on the site** (Chinese included — the hei/gothic system fonts are the CJK equivalent of the sans discipline; never a song/serif face).

### Hierarchy
- **Display** (600, `clamp(2.25rem → 4.25rem)`, line-height 1.04, tracking `-0.035em`): The hero H1 only. One per page, ≤16ch wide, always a plain-words statement of what the studio does.
- **Headline** (600, `clamp(1.625rem → 2.5rem)`, line-height 1.12): Section headers (`h2`). Drops to a 26px floor on mobile.
- **Title** (600, `1.375rem`, line-height 1.25): Card and subsection headings (`h3`), plan names, FAQ summaries. **Title Sm** (`1.125rem`) is the same role scaled down inside dense cards.
- **Body** (400, `1.0625rem`/17px, line-height 1.6): Default reading text. **Body Lg** (`1.25rem`) is the lede, in Ink Muted, capped ~38rem; supporting paragraphs cap around 44–46rem. **Body Ui** (`1rem`, 600) is button and control text.
- **Body Sm** (400, `0.9375rem`/15px) and **Caption** (`0.875rem`/14px): The supporting tier — card body, list items, kickers, muted notes. **Caption Sm** (`0.8125rem`/13px) is the smallest reading size, for disclaimers and meta rows.
- **Label** (700, `0.75rem`, tracking `0.16em`, UPPERCASE): The eyebrow above every section header, footer column heads, showcase/all-in labels. Steel blue on light, amber on charcoal. **Micro** (`0.6875rem`, tracking `0.12em`) is the same treatment for badges (plan badge, `9:16` format tag).
- **Numeric** (600, Space Grotesk, `3rem` / `2.75rem` in the stat strip, tracking `-0.03em`, line-height 1): Prices, the all-in total, and the stat-strip figures. Given deliberately more weight than body — data is allowed to feel prominent here in a way the sister brand avoids.

**The scale** is a 1px ladder in `rem` (`0.0625rem` steps): 10px (placeholder-caption floor only) · 11 · 12 · 13 · 14 · 15 · 16 · 17 · 18 · 20 · 22px for text, then `1.375 / 1.5 / 1.625 / 1.75rem` fixed heads (24 / 26 / 27 / 28px, used as inline `h2`/`h3` overrides inside cards and as the mobile `h2` floor), and two `clamp()` display sizes: **page-title** `clamp(2.25rem, 4.4vw, 3.5rem)` for interior-page `h1`, **display** `clamp(2.25rem, 5.2vw, 4.25rem)` for the hero. Stay on a ladder step; don't introduce an in-between size.

### Named Rules
**The Money Rule.** Any dollar figure — plan price, per-video rate, recommended ad spend, all-in total — is set in Space Grotesk at numeric weight, never in muted grey, and the fee, the spend, and the total always travel together. A contractor who has to hunt for the real number assumes he is being handled.

**The Plain-Words Rule.** Every headline says what the studio does in plain language. No riffing on the company name, no "Busy Season" puns — the name is already doing enough explaining. Holds in Chinese: 直说做什么，不玩公司名的文字游戏.

**The CJK Reset Rule.** On `:lang(zh)`, negative letter-spacing is set back to `normal` everywhere (tight tracking mangles Han characters), body line-height rises to `1.75`, the uppercase-label tracking drops to `~0.04em`, and every `ch`-based `max-width` cap is re-tuned (one Han glyph ≈ 1ch, so the English caps run far too wide). The wordmark "Busy Season" stays in its Latin form — the registered name is not translated.

## Layout

A single centred column, `max-width: 80rem` (1280px), with `0 1.5rem` (24px) gutters; full-bleed is permitted only for portfolio and video showcase moments. Narrow prose (legal pages, form columns) caps at `46rem`. The spacing rhythm is an 8px base grid — every padding and gap value is a multiple of it.

Section vertical padding is `7rem` (112px) desktop, tightening to `4rem` (64px) below 860px; a `.section-tight` variant uses `5rem`. Content grids are 2/3/4-column on desktop (`.grid-2/3/4`) with a `1.5rem` gap, collapsing to a single column at 860px. The hero and other split layouts use an asymmetric `1.15fr 0.85fr` (text-heavy) split that stacks at 1000px.

Three breakpoints: **1000px** (hero and split grids stack, portfolio drops to 2-up, footer to 2-col), **860px** (sections tighten, all content grids collapse to one column, the all-in callout restacks as fee → spend → total), **760px** (primary nav collapses into a toggled dropdown that includes the CTA and language toggle). Mobile-first is the priority order even though the source is written desktop-down.

### Bilingual structure
English lives at the root (`/index.html`, `/pricing.html`, …); Simplified Chinese is a parallel static set under `/zh/` (`/zh/index.html`, …) sharing the one `styles.css` and `script.js` via `../`. English pages carry `<html lang="en">`, Chinese pages `<html lang="zh-Hans">`, with reciprocal `<link rel="alternate" hreflang>` tags (`en`, `zh-Hans`, `x-default`→en). No auto-redirect and no persisted preference — the toggle is plain links, and outreach sends the visitor whichever URL fits. The two legal pages (`terms.html`, `privacy.html`) stay English-only with a short Chinese note; there is no `/zh/terms.html`.

## Elevation & Depth

**Flat by default; a shadow means something was deliberately lifted.** At rest, every card, plan, input, and panel is a flat white (or charcoal-soft) surface defined by a 1px hairline border. Depth in the resting state is tonal — charcoal sections advancing against paper, charcoal-soft cards against charcoal — not cast by shadows.

Two shadows exist, both soft, wide, and downward (negative spread so they read as a lift rather than a drop):

### Shadow Vocabulary
- **Lift** (`box-shadow: 0 18px 40px -24px rgba(22, 24, 28, 0.45)`): The featured Growth plan carries this permanently to pull it forward of the Launch Pack; also available as `.card-lift` for a card that must stand out.
- **Lift (raised)** (`box-shadow: 0 24px 56px -24px rgba(22, 24, 28, 0.5)`): The hover state for portfolio thumbnails, paired with a `scale(1.02)`.

### Named Rules
**The Flat-At-Rest Rule.** Surfaces are flat with a hairline border by default. A shadow appears only as (a) permanent emphasis on the single featured pricing plan, or (b) a response to hover on a portfolio thumbnail. Adding a shadow anywhere else breaks the system.

## Shapes

Crisp, near-square geometry — sharper than the sister brand's soft rounding, and the sharpness is intentional. The corner scale is small: **4px** for the smallest chips and badges (plan badge, `9:16` format tag, per-unit pill, raw-photo tiles); **6px** (`--radius`) for buttons, inputs, and stage steps; **10px** (`--radius-lg`) for the larger containers — cards, plans, the reel frame, portfolio thumbnails, the all-in callout. Focus outlines round at 2px. Chips and circular marks (guarantee check, play button, sound toggle, showcase arrow) are the only fully-round elements — pills at `999px`, marks at `50%`.

Borders are the primary definition tool: a single 1px hairline (`#e2e2e0` on light, `rgba(242,242,240,0.14)` on charcoal). The featured plan escalates to a 2px steel-blue border. Numbered steps use a top border only — 2px ink on light, amber on charcoal — as a rule under the step number. The raw-photo tiles in the before/after showcase get a deliberate `±1deg` rotation so the grid looks like snapshots dropped on a table, not a polished asset.

## Components

### Buttons
- **Shape:** 6px corners (`--radius`), 1px border, `min-height: 3rem` (≥44px touch target), `0.875rem 1.75rem` padding, 600 weight.
- **Primary** (`.btn-primary`): Work-Light Amber fill with **near-black (`#1a1b1e`) label text**, per the contrast rule — never white. One per page.
- **Hover / Focus:** Background shifts to Amber Pressed (`#d4741d`) over 130ms. Focus-visible draws a 3px amber outline at 2px offset, sitewide.
- **Secondary** (`.btn-secondary`): Transparent fill, ink text, `rgba(26,27,30,0.28)` border that darkens to solid ink on hover. On charcoal, the text and border flip to on-dark values. Used for the one non-primary action on a page ("See the full process", "Full pricing & FAQ").
- **Inline link** (`.link`): Ink text with a 2px amber underline that shifts to ink on hover.

### Chips
- **Style:** White fill, 1px hairline, `999px` pill, Ink Muted text at `0.8125rem`. On charcoal: charcoal-soft fill, on-dark-dim text.
- **Use:** Format tags on portfolio pieces ("9:16", "HVAC"). Display only — not interactive filters.

### Cards / Containers
- **Corner Style:** 10px (`--radius-lg`).
- **Background:** White on light sections (including `.section-alt`); Charcoal Soft (`#1f2229`) inside charcoal sections.
- **Shadow Strategy:** None at rest — 1px hairline border only. See Elevation.
- **Border:** `#e2e2e0` on light, `rgba(242,242,240,0.14)` on charcoal.
- **Internal Padding:** `2rem` (cards), `2.5rem` (plans).

### Inputs / Fields
- **Style:** White fill, 6px corners, `rgba(26,27,30,0.22)` 1px border, `min-height: 3rem`, `0.75rem 1rem` padding, single-column, generous label above.
- **Hover:** Border darkens to `rgba(26,27,30,0.4)`.
- **Focus:** The global 3px amber outline at 2px offset.
- **Radio cards** (`.radio-card`): Full clickable tile; the selected tile takes an amber border and a `rgba(232,134,46,0.06)` wash. `accent-color` on the input is amber.
- **Success notice:** Left border flips from amber to Guarantee Green (`.notice-success`).

### Navigation
- **Style:** Sticky, `rgba(250,250,249,0.9)` with a 10px backdrop blur, 1px bottom hairline, `4.5rem` tall. Wordmark left ("Busy *Season*" — "Season" in Ink Muted, no icon mark in the header); four text links (Work, Services & Pricing, How It Works, About) in Ink Muted at `0.9375rem`; amber CTA right.
- **States:** Links go to solid ink on hover and for `[aria-current="page"]`. The CTA label ("Get a Free Sample Ad" / "领取免费样片广告") must never wrap.
- **Mobile (≤760px):** Links and CTA collapse into a hamburger-toggled dropdown panel (`.nav-panel` uses `display: contents` on desktop so it is layout-inert there). Escape closes it and returns focus to the toggle.
- **Language toggle** (`.lang-toggle`): `EN / 中文`, grouped immediately left of the CTA inside `.nav-cta-wrap`. Plain `<a>` links, no JavaScript — each page points at its own translated counterpart; the active language is `[aria-current="true"]` in solid ink, the other in Ink Muted, split by a 1px hairline. In the mobile dropdown it stacks with the CTA. Legal pages (English-only, no `/zh/` counterpart) point `中文` at the Chinese homepage.

### Signature Components

**The All-In Cost Callout** (`.allin`). A reusable fee + spend + total object, rendered as a 5-column `1fr auto 1fr auto 1fr` grid with "+" and "=" operators between the three figures (Space Grotesk, Ink Muted). Values are numeric-weight and **deliberately not muted** — grey-on-grey here reads as something being hidden. The total is steel blue on light, amber on charcoal. On mobile it restacks vertically with the operators hidden and hairline rules between rows. A compact `.allin-inline` variant lives inside each pricing card above the button.

**The Guarantee Block** (`.guarantee`). Its own full-width band, `rgba(63,143,95,0.07)` wash between two green hairlines, sized to be seen on a phone without scrolling past it: a green circular check mark, a green headline, one sentence of plain copy (no legalese), and the primary CTA. Risk reversal outranks the rate lock everywhere — this block gets prominent visual treatment; the rate lock never does.

**The Before/After Showcase** (`.showcase`). A `1fr auto 1fr` row: raw client phone photos on the left in a loose 2×2 grid with `±1deg` tile rotation, a circular arrow in the middle, and the finished 9:16 ad frame on the right (charcoal gradient, amber top-glow). The left side stays **unprettified on purpose** — the gap between the snapshot and the finished ad is the sales argument. Stacks vertically at 860px with the arrow rotated 90°.

**The Pricing Pair** (`.pricing` / `.plan`). Two columns, `align-items: start`. The featured plan (Growth) carries a 2px steel-blue border, the permanent Lift shadow, and a steel "Best value" badge notched over its top-left corner. Bullet lists use green check icons. Each card carries its `.allin-inline` note and its CTA pinned to the bottom (`margin-top: auto`) so the two buttons align.

**The Reel Frame** (`.reel` / `.showcase-frame` / `.work-thumb`). The recurring 9:16 vertical container for delivered work. Charcoal gradient ground with a radial amber top-glow, 10px corners, holds a muted autoplaying `<video>` (or an honest text placeholder until real client work exists). A circular bottom-right sound toggle is the only way to hear a clip — it takes an amber fill with near-black icon on hover. Portfolio thumbnails add `scale(1.02)` + raised shadow on hover/focus.

## Do's and Don'ts

### Do:
- **Do** keep one amber action per page and keep amber under ~10% of the screen (The One Voice Rule).
- **Do** set every dollar figure in Space Grotesk at numeric weight, and keep fee + ad spend + all-in total together wherever a price appears (The Money Rule).
- **Do** put near-black text on the amber button; use amber-as-text only on charcoal.
- **Do** reach for charcoal (`#16181c`) when a section needs drama — hero, "what you get", closing CTA, footer — and cool paper everywhere else.
- **Do** define surfaces with a 1px hairline border and keep them flat at rest; reserve the Lift shadow for the featured plan and portfolio hover.
- **Do** use 6px corners for controls and 10px for containers — nothing softer.
- **Do** let raw client photos look raw in the before/after showcase; the contrast is the argument.
- **Do** use real produced-ad footage or an honest labelled placeholder — never stock imagery.
- **Do** keep every new page in both languages: a root `/x.html` gets a `/zh/x.html`, both with reciprocal `hreflang`, or neither ships.
- **Do** reset negative letter-spacing, raise line-height, and re-tune `ch` caps under `:lang(zh)` (The CJK Reset Rule).

### Don't:
- **Don't** set amber type on the light background, and never put white text on the amber button (both fail AA).
- **Don't** introduce a serif anywhere — serif belongs to the sister consumer brand.
- **Don't** mute or grey-out pricing, ad spend, or the all-in total.
- **Don't** add a second competing CTA or a second accent colour to a page.
- **Don't** add shadows to cards, inputs, or sections at rest, and don't soften the 6px/10px corner scale.
- **Don't** add a third background mood — the system is paper or charcoal, nothing between.
- **Don't** pun on the "Busy Season" name in a headline; state what the studio does in plain words.
- **Don't** add confetti, cursor trails, or motion longer than ~150ms.
- **Don't** load a CJK webfont, translate the "Busy Season" wordmark, or set Chinese in a song/serif face.
- **Don't** carry the Latin negative tracking or `ch`-based width caps onto the Chinese pages.
