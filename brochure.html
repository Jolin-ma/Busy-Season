About

Where a scan
becomes a memory.

LegacyLink bridges the physical and digital worlds by embedding durable, weather-resistant QR codes on tombstones, monuments, and urns. A single smartphone scan opens a rich multimedia biography — photos, video, voice recordings, and written stories — accessible by families, genealogists, and future generations forever.

What's Included

— Laser-engraved QR code + personalized name
— Permanent digital memorial profile
— Scan analytics dashboard for family
— Privacy controls — PIN-protect at any time
— Free profile updates forever

Living Legacy — Premium Plan

Richer memories.
Deeper connections.

The Living Legacy upgrade unlocks four powerful features designed to bring families closer and preserve history for generations to come.

01

Collaborative Guestbook

A living digital memorial visitors can contribute to instantly. Scan the QR code to leave a message, share a memory, or upload a photo directly from your phone.

— Family & friends can reply to stories and "heart" memories
— Admin dashboard to approve or invite trusted contributors

02

Geotagging & Cemetery Navigation

The exact GPS coordinates of the gravesite are pinned at setup. Relatives can open Legacy Link and be guided directly to the plot via Google or Apple Maps.

— Syncs with Google Maps & Apple Maps
— Micro-navigation walking path from cemetery gates to the headstone

03

Family Tree Integration

Connect the deceased to an interactive visual family tree — parents, siblings, children, and ancestors. Multiple Legacy Link profiles in the same cemetery can be interlinked, letting visitors walk through generations.

— Compatible with GEDCOM, Ancestry.com & MyHeritage

04

Priority Support

High-touch, rapid-response customer service for families managing the memorial page — especially important during a time of grief.

— Guaranteed response under 2 hours
— Digital onboarding — our team helps upload the biography, photos & timeline
