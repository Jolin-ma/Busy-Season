LEGACY LINK

Preserving Life Stories Through
Digital Memorials

LegacyLink is a modern memorial technology company that bridges the physical and digital worlds.

By placing durable, weather-resistant QR codes on tombstones, monuments, and urns, we allow grieving families, genealogists, and future generations to instantly access a rich, multimedia biography of the deceased via a simple smartphone scan.

Our mission is to ensure that no one’s story is ever forgotten.
