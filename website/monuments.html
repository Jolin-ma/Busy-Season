MONUMENTS & PUBLIC MEMORIALS

Every plaque, bench, and statue
holds a story worth telling.

From civic cenotaphs to memorial benches in public parks, from building cornerstones to historic statues — LegacyLink brings the digital dimension to permanent physical structures.

A visitor scans the QR plaque and is taken directly to a rich multimedia archive: the history of the building, the life of the person honoured, the story behind the monument. No plaque can hold a lifetime — but a LegacyLink QR can.
