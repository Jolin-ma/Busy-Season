About

Built to make produced tribute films the standard, not the exception.

LegacyLink Studio started with a simple observation: the way we keep personal media — photos, video, voice notes — has moved entirely to the phone in our pocket, but the way we honor the people and pets we've lost hasn't caught up to that shift.

Our mission

Turn personal memory into produced, lasting tribute.

We combine AI production — writing, narration, scoring, and editing — with permanent, ownable pages so that a tribute isn't a folder of files but something considered: a short film that actually feels like the life it's honoring.

We started with Loyal Tale because pet loss is often under-served by traditional memorial products, even though it's one of the most universally felt losses people experience. It's also where we could prove the model end to end: production quality, permanence, and a page that grows with the people who cared.

What we build next extends the same idea to other kinds of personal legacy.
