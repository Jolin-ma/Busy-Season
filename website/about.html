About

A small studio with one job.

LegacyLink Studio makes video ads for local service businesses. Not brand films, not campaign strategy — ads that get someone to call you.

Why we exist

Good video used to be out of reach for a business your size.

A professionally produced video ad has traditionally meant a crew, a shoot day, a location, an editor, and an invoice that only makes sense if you're a national brand. So most local businesses either skipped video entirely or filmed something on a phone in the truck and hoped for the best.

AI production changes that arithmetic. It removes the crew day and the equipment cost, which is exactly the part that put a five-figure floor under the whole thing. What's left is the work that actually decides whether an ad performs — the angle, the hook, the offer, the pacing — and that's where our time goes.

That's the entire reason a finished ad from us costs $200 instead of $5,000. We're not cutting corners on the thinking. We're not paying for a camera truck.

How we work

Four things we've decided on purpose.

We only sell creative

We don't want the keys to your ad account. You get the files; you keep control of your spend. It keeps us honest and keeps our scope narrow enough to do well.

Prices are on the website

Both packages, in full, with the per-video maths shown. If you have to book a call to find out what something costs, that's a tactic, not a service.

We'd rather show than pitch

For a small number of businesses we'll produce a sample ad before you've committed to anything. Reacting to a real video beats reading a proposal.

We'll tell you if we're wrong for you

Some businesses don't need more video — they need a better offer, or a phone that gets answered. We'd rather say that on the first email than take a deposit.

Who's behind it

An independent, owner-run studio.

LegacyLink Studio is small and independent. When you email, you're talking to the person who produces your ads — not an account manager relaying messages to a production team.

That's a genuine advantage at this size: nothing gets lost between your brief and the edit. It also means we take on a limited number of clients at a time, which is why Growth includes a reserved production slot.

Founder background

A fuller introduction will be added here as the studio grows. In the meantime, the most useful thing we can show you isn't a bio — it's a video made for your business.
