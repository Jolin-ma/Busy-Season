FAQ

Questions we get asked first.

Do you run the ads for us?

No. We produce the video creative and hand you the finished files. What you do with them — post organically, run them as paid ads yourself, or pass them to your marketing person — is entirely up to you. We deliberately don't take control of client ad accounts.

Why is Growth cheaper per video than Starter?

Because a two-video batch carries proportionally more of the one-time cost of taking on a new client — the intake, the first brief, learning your business — than an eight-video month does. Once we know your business, each additional video costs us less to make, and the price reflects that.

We'd rather pass that on than invent a reason to charge more for subscribing. Subscribing is simply the cheaper option per video.

What if we don't like the first draft?

Every video includes one revision round. You tell us what isn't landing — the hook, the pacing, the offer, the look — and we recut it. Before that, we send a short concept or script for each video so you can redirect it early, while changes are cheap.

If you want more than one revision round on a video, ask — we'd rather quote it than pretend the limit doesn't exist.

How fast do we get our videos?

A Starter batch typically lands within about a week of the brief being confirmed. Growth clients get batches of 4 on a biweekly schedule, agreed up front. We'll give you a firm delivery date when you book rather than a vague estimate.

What do you need from us to start?

A short brief: your business and location, the service you want advertised, who your ideal customer is, one clear selling point or offer, any logo or existing photos and footage you have, and which formats you need. It takes about ten minutes. Anything you explicitly don't want — competitor comparisons, a certain music style — tell us there too.

Who owns the finished videos?

You get the finished files to use in your marketing. The exact terms — including whether we may show the work in our own portfolio — are set out in a short written agreement before production starts, so nobody is guessing later. See usage rights, and ask us if you need something specific.

How does payment work?

Starter is 50% up front before production starts, with the balance due on delivery. Growth is 50% up front on the first month, then standard monthly invoicing once we're working together.

Prices are in Canadian dollars and no tax is added, so the figure you agree to is the figure on the invoice.

These are launch rates — will my price go up?

Not if you're on Growth. The monthly rate you start at is the rate you keep, for as long as the retainer runs without a break — if we raise prices for new clients later, yours doesn't move. Starter is a one-off batch, so each batch is priced at whatever the rate is when you order it.

One honest exception: we're not registered for GST/HST today, so nothing is added to your invoice. If we cross the threshold where registration becomes mandatory, tax would start applying to work done after that point, including on a running retainer. We'd tell you before it showed up on a bill. Your fee wouldn't change — the tax would sit on top of it.

We're not a roofer. Will you work with us?

Probably. Roofing and contracting is where we're focused, because those businesses live or die on inbound calls and rarely have video capability in-house. The same approach works for HVAC, plumbing, landscaping, and similar local trades — get in touch and we'll tell you straight whether we're a good fit for you.
