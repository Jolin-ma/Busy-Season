Your ad account

Your account stays yours.

To launch your campaign we need access to your Meta ad account. Here's exactly what that means, because handing a stranger access to anything should come with a straight answer.

  • Campaign access only. You add us as a partner in Meta Business Manager, and we use it to build and launch your campaign.
  • Your card never leaves your account. Meta bills you directly. We can't see or touch your payment method.
  • You can remove us any time from your own Business settings, without asking us first.

FAQ

Questions we get asked first.

Do you launch the ads for us?

Yes. We build the campaign on your own ad account — targeting, audience, budget, where the leads land — launch it, and walk you through how to read it. Two weeks in, we check how it's doing.

This is the part most video people won't do. Finished files sitting in a folder don't get anyone to call you.

What access do you need to my ad account?

Campaign access only. You add us as a partner in Meta Business Manager, and we use that access to build and launch your campaign. Your card stays on your account and we never see it.

You can remove our access yourself, any time, from your Business settings — no need to ask us.

Who pays for the ads?

You do, directly to Meta, on your own ad account with your own card. We never take custody of your ad budget — we hold partner access to build and launch the campaign, and that's all. The account, the spend history, and the audience data stay with you.

How much should I spend on ads?

At least $500 for a first test.

The honest reason for a floor: below that, the campaign never gathers enough data to tell what's working. You'd be guessing, and the test would fail for reasons that have nothing to do with the creative. If that's out of reach right now, tell us — we'd rather say "not yet" than take the money.

What's it cost all in?

$750 to us, plus $500 or more in ad spend paid to Meta — about $1,250 to get started. That's the whole number; nothing renews after it.

Do I need a Facebook page or ad account already?

No. If you don't have them, we'll walk you through setting them up — a Facebook page, a Business Manager, and an ad account with your card on it. It takes about twenty minutes and you own all of it afterwards.

Is this AI?

Yes, partly — and here's exactly which part. We use AI for production: the hook, the camera moves, transitions, b-roll, atmosphere, the text treatment, the polish that makes phone footage look like it cost money.

What we don't do is invent your work. The houses, the crew, the trucks, the before-and-afters are yours — real photos of real jobs you actually did. A homeowner deciding on a five-figure job is making a trust call, and a generic render doesn't earn it the way your own work does.

That's why we ask for 10–20 job photos before we start. It's the difference between an ad that looks like your business and a render that looks like anyone's.

What if I don't like the first drafts?

On a Launch Pack, your deposit is refunded in full. No argument, no partial credit — if the first cuts aren't what you wanted, you get your money back.

Before that point you'll have seen a short script or shot concept for each video, so there's a cheap chance to redirect us early. The Launch Pack also includes one revision round.

How long am I locked in?

You're not. The Launch Pack is a one-off — nothing renews, nothing auto-charges, and there's no contract to get out of.

How fast do I get my ads?

About two weeks from the day your photos and footage land with us — not from the day you signed. Asset collection is the step that decides both the timeline and the quality, so we'll chase you for it.

What if I already run my own ads?

Then you can drop the three new videos straight into the campaign you're already running on your own account. If you'd rather we set up a fresh campaign alongside it, we'll do that instead.

Can you keep running the campaign for me?

Yes, if you'd like us to. After launch the campaign is yours to run, and plenty of owners are happy doing that. If you'd rather hand it off, we'll quote ongoing management for your account and budget, agreed in writing before any work starts.

How does payment work?

$375 deposit to start — refundable under the guarantee — and $375 on delivery.

Both are by Interac e-Transfer, and card payment is available if you'd rather. Nothing is kept on file and nothing charges again.

Ad spend never runs through us — that's billed to your card by Meta, on your own account.
