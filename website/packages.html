Premium Features — Living Legacy

Richer memories.
Deeper connections.

The Living Legacy upgrade unlocks four powerful features designed to bring families closer and preserve history for generations to come.

01

Collaborative Guestbook

A living digital memorial that visitors can contribute to instantly. Scan the QR code to leave a message, share a memory, or upload a photo directly from your phone — no account required.

—Family & friends can reply to stories and "heart" memories
—Admin dashboard to approve or invite trusted contributors

02

Location Pinning & Sharing

Stand at the memorial site once to capture the exact GPS coordinates. Family members who have never been there get a one-tap link that opens the precise spot in their maps app — no searching, no guessing.

—Opens in Google Maps or Apple Maps
—Copy a share link — paste into a text, email, or the guestbook
—Optional visitor note for directions GPS can't give ("past the oak tree, north entrance")

Family Legacy Suite

Four connected tools that together build a complete family record — not just a chart, but the full story behind it.

—Direct lineage tree — collapsible generations, portrait photos, "life in a sentence" bios
—Family story editor — origin narrative, traditions, and defining milestone timeline
—Heirloom gallery — document each object with sepia photo, era, and provenance
—Document archive — letters, certificates & photographs organized by decade

04

Priority Support

High-touch, rapid-response customer service for families managing the memorial page — especially important during a time of grief.

—Guaranteed response under 2 hours
