How It Works

What working with us actually looks like.

Six steps from first email to finished files. You're involved in two of them, and neither takes more than a few minutes of your day.

Intake

You fill in a short brief — your business, the service you want advertised, who you want calling, one clear offer, and any logo or footage you already have. About ten minutes. It's the only homework in the whole process, and everything we make is built from it.

Concept

We write a short script or shot concept for each video and send it over before anything is produced. This is your cheapest chance to redirect us — say "that's not how we talk about our warranty" here and it costs nothing to change.

Production

We produce the ads with an AI video pipeline. That's what makes this affordable: no crew day, no location fee, no equipment rental sitting inside your invoice.

Our own QA pass

Before anything reaches you, we watch it back properly — pacing, the hook in the first two seconds, whether your offer is legible on a phone with the sound off. You should never be the first person to notice a problem with your ad.

Your review

You watch the cut and tell us what to change. One revision round per video is included in every package. Want more than that on a particular video? Ask, and we'll quote it rather than pretend the limit doesn't exist.

Delivery

Final files by download link, in the formats you need — 9:16 vertical, 1:1 square, and 16:9 widescreen on request. No portal to log into and no proprietary player. They're your files.

The intake brief

Here's exactly what we'll ask you.

No surprises and no discovery workshop. If you can answer these seven things, we can start.

Start the brief
  • 1. Business name & where you operate
  • 2. The core service you want advertised
  • 3. Your ideal customer — homeowners, a certain age range, urgency signals like storm damage
  • 4. One clear selling point or current offer — "free inspection," "financing available"
  • 5. Any photos, footage, or a logo you can send us
  • 6. Which platforms and formats you need
  • 7. Anything you explicitly don't want — competitor comparisons, certain music styles

Timing

When your videos land.

A Starter batch of two videos typically lands within about a week of the brief being confirmed. We'll give you a firm date when you book, not a range.

Growth clients get four videos every two weeks on an agreed schedule — eight a month, arriving steadily rather than in one lump at month's end, so your creative doesn't go stale halfway through.

Ready when you are.

Start with the brief. If we're not the right fit for your business, we'll say so on the first reply.
