Privacy Policy

⚠ Draft content — NEEDS LEGAL REVIEW. This page is a structural and tonal draft only. It must be reviewed by a qualified attorney before any paying client is taken on.

Last updated: 13 August 2026

This policy covers the LegacyLink Studio website at legacylinkstudio.com. It is short because this site does very little with your data.

1. This site sets no tracking cookies

We do not set advertising or tracking cookies, and we do not currently run analytics on this site. There is no cookie banner because there is nothing to consent to.

The site loads web fonts from Google Fonts. Your browser's request for those font files reaches Google's servers, which is outside our control and covered by Google's own privacy policy.

The site is hosted on Vercel, which processes standard server request data (including IP address) to serve pages and protect the service, as any web host does.

2. The quote form

Submitting the "Get a Quote" form sends what you typed to our own endpoint on this site, which forwards it to us as an email. We collect only the fields on the form: your business name, your name, your email address, and — if you choose to give them — your phone number, service area, which package you're interested in, and what you want the ads to do.

We do not collect your IP address, set a cookie, or profile you when you submit. The message is delivered by Resend, our email provider, and then held in our email account.

If that delivery fails, the page offers to open the same details as a pre-filled message in your own email application instead. That path sends nothing anywhere until you press send yourself.

3. What we do with enquiry information

We use it to:

  • reply to you and quote for the work you asked about;
  • keep a simple record of client and prospect details so we can manage projects we're engaged on.

We do not sell your information, and we do not add you to a marketing list without you asking for it.

4. Client project material

If you engage us, we hold the material you send for production — your brief, logo, photos, and any footage — for as long as needed to produce and support your videos. Whether we may show finished work in our own portfolio is agreed separately in writing; see usage rights.

5. Service providers

This site is hosted by Vercel, and quote-form submissions are delivered by Resend.

Beyond that we use third-party tools to run the studio — email, file delivery, invoicing, and AI video production tools. Material you send us may be processed by those tools for the purpose of producing your videos. We choose providers with reasonable security practices, but we don't control their systems.

6. Retention

We keep enquiry correspondence and project records for as long as there is a business reason to, and for any period required for tax or legal purposes. Ask us to delete your information and we will, unless we're required to keep it.

7. Your rights

Depending on where you live, you may have the right to ask for a copy of the information we hold about you, to have it corrected, or to have it deleted. Email us and we'll deal with it.

8. Children

This is a business-to-business service. It isn't directed at children and we don't knowingly collect their information.

9. Changes

If we add analytics or any other tracking to this site, we'll update this page before doing so.

10. Contact

Privacy questions: info@legacylinkstudio.com.
