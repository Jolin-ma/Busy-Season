Why this matters

Memory is becoming a digital-first medium — memorialization hasn't caught up.

Most of what we keep of the ones we love now lives on a phone or in a cloud account, not in a physical place we can return to. At the same time, our relationships with pets and family are more central to how people define their lives than ever before.

LegacyLink Studio sits at that intersection: we use AI production — writing, narration, scoring, and editing — to turn scattered personal media into something as considered as a produced short film, paired with a permanent, ownable page built to last well beyond any one device.
